Check-in

Five questions on what this lesson builds on, from quick to demanding. You answer by dragging. They do not count toward any grade, and you can submit without answering all of them. This copy of the check-in adds a table of what is recorded and a button that fills in correct answers.

The check-in itself is the first step of Before class. Each question is drawn with different numbers every time the page loads.
